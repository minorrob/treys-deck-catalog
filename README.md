# Trey's Deck Catalog (mobile)

A phone-first view of Trey's Commander deck catalog: 29 ranked designs (6 of them the live decks), the 767-card bench, and three cheat sheets for the table.

**Open it:** https://minorrob.github.io/treys-deck-catalog/

- Tap the deck name at the top to search and switch decks, or jump to a cheat sheet.
- Each deck has three tabs: Overview, Cards and Stats. Tap any card for its details and the other designs that run it.
- Cheat sheets: the D1 Tymna + Kraum play sheet, a mechanics glossary, and priority and the stack.

Direct links: `#D1` opens a deck (`#D1.cards` its card list), `#PLAY-D1` the D1 play sheet, `#GLOSSARY`, `#STACK`, `#BENCH`.

The page is a single self-contained `index.html` (data and card images embedded). Prices are TCGplayer market via Scryfall on Oct 8, 2026.
