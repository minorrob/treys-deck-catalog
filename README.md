# Trey's Deck Catalog (mobile)

A phone-first view of Trey's Commander deck catalog: 29 ranked designs, the six live decks, and the 767-card bench.

**Open it:** https://minorrob.github.io/treys-deck-catalog/

- Tap the deck name at the top to search and switch decks; the arrows step through them in catalog order.
- Each deck has three tabs: Overview, Cards and Stats. Tap any card for its details and the other designs that run it.
- The Bench has search, filters (color, rarity, type, mana value, use) and sorting.

Deep links work: `#D2-A` opens a deck, `#D2-A.cards` its card list, `#BENCH` the bench.

The page is a single self-contained `index.html` (data and card images embedded). Prices are TCGplayer market via Scryfall on Oct 8, 2026.
