# Trey's Deck Catalog (mobile)

A phone-first view of Trey's Commander deck catalog: the 6 active decks, 33 designs grouped as in the master (Set 0, Set 1, Set 2, Group 0, Group 1, Crown Jewels, Hidden Gems), the bench, and the trading pages.

**Open it:** https://minorrob.github.io/treys-deck-catalog/

- Tap the deck name at the top to search and switch decks, or jump to a page.
- Each deck has three tabs: Overview, Cards and Stats. D1 (and its Set 0 twin) adds a fourth, Playsheet, with the Tymna + Kraum loyalty, chains and card notes. Tap any card for its details and the other designs that run it.
- **Sell:** the spare copies a store buys one at a time (singles and low picks), with market value and a store-credit range at 70% to 80% of market.
- **Buy:** Mechs (what to look for in the boxes, by mechanic and color, with example cards) and Singles (specific cards to pick up, including the Group 0 commanders).
- **Giveaway:** the spare bulk commons, uncommons and rares, with one copy of each kept home. Friends search or filter by color, tick cards, set quantities and tap E-mail Asks to send the request.
- Cheat sheets: a mechanics glossary, and priority and the stack (text links under the menu buttons).

Direct links: `#D1` opens a deck (`#G0-D1.cards` its card list), `#D1.playsheet`, `#SELL`, `#BUY`, `#GIVE`, `#BENCH`, `#GLOSSARY`, `#STACK`. The old `#PLAY-D1` link still opens the D1 Playsheet.

The page is a single self-contained `index.html` (data and card images embedded). Prices are TCGplayer market via Scryfall (Oct 8, 2026; Group 0, Sell, Giveaway and the bench refreshed Oct 11, 2026).
